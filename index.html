919 Golf Co. — four design directions

Four takes on the same content. A, B, and C keep your current green-and-gold palette and vary the layout and typography. D deliberately throws the palette out to show what a cleaner, more minimal direction looks like — nothing here is locked in, including the colors. These are working web pages: open each, resize the window, and try them on your phone.

About the photos: these use the one hero photo you already have. Every layout is built to hold up with very little photography, leaning on type, color, and spacing instead — so nothing looks broken today, and all three get noticeably better as you add real event shots.

What to tell us after you look

  1. Which one feels most like your business? Gut reaction is fine — don't overthink it.
  2. Do you actually want to keep green and gold? D shows what happens without them. No wrong answer.
  3. Which one would your ideal customer respond to? That may not be the same as your favorite.
  4. Anything you want to steal across directions? Mixing is completely fine — the packages from one, the colors from another.
  5. Anything that feels wrong for the brand? Just as useful to know.
  6. What photos can you get? Even a few good shots of a real event change what's possible.
