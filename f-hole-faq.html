Frequently asked

Your questions, answered

Everything people usually ask before booking. Still unsure about something? Just reach out.

What's included?
We set up and tear down before and after the event, and provide an attendant for the full duration. Adult right- and left-handed clubs are provided, plus a youth set for our young golf prodigies.
What courses and game modes are available?
Through GSPro software, guests can play dozens of the world's most famous courses — including Pebble Beach, St. Andrews, TPC Sawgrass, and Augusta National. Game modes include stroke play, closest-to-pin, longest drive, and custom scramble formats.
What is your cancellation policy?
Cancellations 14 or more business days before the event are 100% refundable. Between 7 and 14 days prior, you receive a 50% refund with the remaining 50% credited toward a future booking. Within 7 days, you receive 100% credit toward your next booking.
What happens if the weather is bad?
Our simulators are weather permitting, as rain and severe weather can damage the equipment. If conditions aren't suitable we'll gladly reschedule at no additional cost. We can also set up indoors, provided the space fits our footprint of 17'W × 15'L × 10'H.
Do you require a deposit?
A 50% deposit is required for The Tee Time and The Scramble. The Invitational requires 25%. Your deposit locks in the date and counts toward your total.
How can we get in touch?
Every inquiry is followed up with a call, text, or email as soon as possible. Prefer not to fill out a form? Email us and we'll respond as quickly as we can.
Why 919 Golf Co. instead of a big franchise?
As a locally owned small business, we take pride in serving the Triangle. From your first inquiry to the final swing, we're committed to clear communication, reliable service, and a seamless setup. We're your neighbors, and every event matters to us.
