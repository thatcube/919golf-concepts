What happens next

No pressure and no obligation — an inquiry just starts the conversation.

  • We reply in 1–2 days with custom pricing for your date and location.
  • A 50% deposit holds your date for the Tee Time and Scramble packages, 25% for the Invitational.
  • Your deposit counts toward the total. The balance is due 7 days before the event.
  • Free rescheduling if the weather doesn't cooperate.
  • We handle everything — delivery, setup, an attendant for the full event, and teardown.
